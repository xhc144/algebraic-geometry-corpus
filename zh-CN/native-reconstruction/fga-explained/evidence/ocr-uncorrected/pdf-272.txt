(9.3.10.1) q: Hom(Q,N) > f.(F @ f*N).

The pair (Q,q) is unique, up to unique isomorphism, and by [EGAIII2, 7.7.9],
forming it commutes with changing the base, in particular, with localizing.

(iv) is a special case of (iii), Conversely, (iv) implies (iii) by [EGAIII2, 7.7.10]
or [OB72, Cor.5.1 p.72]; this useful implication is known as the “property of
