REMARK 8.3.5. It is easy to generalize 8.3.2 to the case Y is quasi-compact
and f is quasi-compact and quasi-separated (in the last part of the argument,
the intersections Uj,...;,. are only quasi-compact, and one has to replace the Cech
complex by a suitable “hyper Cech” variant).
