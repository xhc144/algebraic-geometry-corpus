The following fact is very important.

PROPOSITION 1.12 ([EGAIV2, Proposition 2.4.6]). A flat morphism that is
locally of finite presentation is open.

This is not true in general for flat morphisms that are not locally of finite
presentation; however, a weaker version of this fact holds.

PROPOSITION 1.13 ([EGAIV2, Corollaire 2.3.12]). If f: X — Y isa faithfully
flat surjective quasi-compact morphism, a subset of Y is open if and only if its
inverse image in X is open in X.

In other words, Y has the topology induced by that of X.
