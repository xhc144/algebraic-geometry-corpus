This example is particularly simple, because it is defined by a functor. In most
of the cases that. we are interested in, the sheaves on a given object will be defined

difficulties, and forces one to use the unpleasant machinery of pseudo-functors. On
the other hand, this discrepancy between the topology on the base and the topology
in which the sheaves are defined is what makes descent theory for quasi-coherent
sheaves so much more than an exercise in formalism.
