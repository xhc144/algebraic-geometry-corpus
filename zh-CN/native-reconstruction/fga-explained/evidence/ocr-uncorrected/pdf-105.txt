4.3.3. Descent via ample invertible sheaves. Descent for affine morphism
can be very useful, but is obviously limited in scope. One is more easily interested
in projective morphisms, rather than in affine ones. Descent works in this case, as
long as the projective morphisms are equipped with ample invertible sheaves, and
these also come with descent data.
