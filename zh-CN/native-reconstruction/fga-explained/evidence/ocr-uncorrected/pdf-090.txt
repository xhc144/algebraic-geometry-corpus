Since two equivalent topologies on the same category have the same sieves, we
obtain the following generalization of Proposition 2.49.

PROPOSITION 4.16. Let C a category, T and T’ two topologies on C, F > C
a fibered category. Suppose that 7’ is subordinate to T. If F is a prestack, or a
stack, relative to JT, then it is also a prestack, or a stack, relative to 7’.

In particular, if T and T’ are equivalent, then F is a stack relative to T if and
only if it is also a stack relative to T’.
