THEOREM 5.26. Let S be a noetherian scheme, and let X — S be a strongly
quasi-projective morphism. Let f : R — X xg X be a schematic equivalence
relation on X over S, such that the projections f,, fo : R = X are proper and
flat. Then a schematic quotient X — Q exists over S. Moreover, the quotient is
effective, the morphism X — Q is faithfully flat and strongly projective, and Q is
strongly quasi-projective over S.
