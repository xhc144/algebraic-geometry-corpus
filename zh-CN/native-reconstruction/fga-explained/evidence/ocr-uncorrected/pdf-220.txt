REMARKS 8.4.11.

(a) The main theorems in §8.2,8.4 are analogous to the results of Serre on the
comparison between algebraic and analytic geometry (GAGA). See [Ser56]
and [SGA1, XII].

(b) The results of §8.2,8.4 have been generalized by Knutson to algebraic spaces
[Knu71, chap. V]. A generalization to stacks (Deligne-Mumford’s stacks, or
Artin’s stacks [Art74b], [LMB00}) has been recently worked out. For a gen-
eralization of Zariski’s main theorem (8.2.17), see [LMBO0, 16.5]. A general-
ization to Artin stacks of the finiteness theorem for proper morphisms has been
given by Faltings [Fal03], and, independently by Olsson [Ols], who deduces
it from a Chow’s lemma for Artin stacks. Olsson (loc. cit.) also generalizes
Grothendieck’s main existence theorem 8.4.2 to Artin stacks.
