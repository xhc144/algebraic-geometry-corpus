4.4. Descent along torsors

One of the most interesting examples of descent is descent for quasi-coherent
sheaves along fpqe torsors. This can be considered as a vast generalization of the
well-known equivalence between the category of real vector spaces and the category
of complex vector spaces with an anti-linear involution. Torsors are generalizations
of principal fiber bundles in topology; and I always find it striking that among the
simplest examples of torsors are Galois field extensions (see Example 4.45).
